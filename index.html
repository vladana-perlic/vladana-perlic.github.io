I research, write, translate, publish, collect prizes, and make jokes precisely when adults would advise against it. This site gathers the receipts: AI papers, books, awarded poems, translations, and interviews.

About

I write poems and study machines that need to lay off the shrooms

I am an Industrial PhD candidate at STMicroelectronics and Télécom Paris, working on LLMs, retrieval-augmented generation, agentic workflows, and multimodal technical documentation. In less CV-shaped terms: I study how AI systems find information, assemble answers, improvise when they should not, and sound frighteningly calm while doing it.

I also write poetry, prose, and essays. The overlap is less strange than it sounds: I spend a lot of time asking what a sentence is doing, what it is leaving out, and whether there is evidence behind the confidence.

(The awards and publications are listed below, just in case you need proof that the jokes come with academic follow-through.)

Outside of work, my hobbies include skateboarding, snowboarding, surfing, and anything that might result in a serious brain injury.

The rest of my worldview can be triangulated from my personal heroes: Nikola Tesla, that Chinese lady who surfskates in heels, and Shrek.

Location

Anthology / Ukrainian translation

Vladana Perlić’s poems appear in Ukrainian in Sun in the Water

September 2026 / Lviv, Ukraine

Ten poems by Vladana Perlić have been published in Ukrainian translation in Sun in the Water («Сонце у воді»), an anthology of contemporary Croatian and Bosnian-Herzegovinian poetry printed in September 2026 by the Lviv publisher Piramida, in publisher Vasyl Gabor’s collection. Selected and edited by the Croatian writer Siniša Matasović, who also wrote the foreword, the book brings together fifteen poets, each represented by ten poems.

The anthology grew out of years of collaboration and mutual visits between Croatian and Ukrainian poets. At the suggestion of translator Maria Shun, who spent part of her career in the United States in daily contact with intellectuals from Croatia and Bosnia and Herzegovina, several prominent poets from Bosnia and Herzegovina joined the Croatian selection.

The anthology was presented on 13 September 2026 at the Pivden Cultural Hub (Культурний хаб Південь) as part of the Lviv BookForum, Ukraine’s largest and oldest book fair, held since 1994. Under the title Poetic Voices of Croatia, translator Alla Tatarenko and editor Siniša Matasović introduced the book to Ukrainian audiences with a presentation and poetry reading. During the festival (11–13 September), the anthology was also presented at the Department of Slavic Philology of the Ivan Franko National University of Lviv, founded in 1661. The editor attended in person alongside the publisher and translators; most of the other poets took part through recorded video readings of their own poems, while the translators read the Ukrainian versions live.

The poems were translated by Ukrainian professors and scholars Alla Tatarenko, Mariana Komarytsia, and Maria Shun, with the foreword translated by Anna Komarytsia. Simultaneous interpretation at the presentations was provided by Dariya Pavlešen and Katarina Jovakarić of the Department of Ukrainian Language and Literature at the University of Zagreb’s Faculty of Humanities and Social Sciences, and the Croatian delegation’s visit to Lviv was supported by IDEMO – Institute for Democracy, Zagreb.

With this publication, Ukrainian joins the languages Vladana’s poetry has been translated into, alongside English, French, German, Italian, Polish, Hungarian, and Hindi.

Also in the anthology: Krešimir Bagić, Tomica Bajsić, Boris Domagoj Biletić, Tomislav Marijan Bilosnić, Lana Derkač, Sanja Domenuš, Ivan Herceg, Žarko Jovanovski, Senka Marić, Siniša Matasović, Semezdin Mehmedinović, Melida Travančić, Darija Žilić, and Joso Živković.

Author bio

Short author bio, ready to paste into festival programmes, anthologies, and journals.

This bio covers my literary work only. For research, see the Scholar CV.

Srpski

Vladana Perlić objavila je dvije zbirke poezije: „Kucanje na vrata kule“ (Zaprokul, 2020) i „Isus među dojkama“ (LOM, 2020).

Dobitnica je Q21 umjetničke rezidencije u Beču za 2022, kao i brojnih nagrada, od kojih su najznačajnije: „Novica Tadić“ (2020), „Aladin Lukač“ (2021), „Anka Topić“ (2021), „Ratkovićeve večeri poezije“ (2020), „Ulaznica 2021“, „Mak Dizdar“ (2018. i 2020), „Milo Bošković“ (2020. i 2023) i „Natječaj Gradske knjižnice Solin“ (2025).

Zastupljena je u Antologiji suvremene hrvatske i bosanskohercegovačke poezije „Sunce u vodi“ (ukr. «Сонце у воді»), 2026.

Pjesme su joj prevedene na engleski, francuski, njemački, italijanski, poljski, mađarski, ukrajinski i hindi.

Živi i radi u Francuskoj.

English

Vladana Perlić has published two poetry collections: Knocking on the Tower Door (Kucanje na vrata kule, Zaprokul, 2020) and Jesus Among Breasts (Isus među dojkama, LOM, 2020).

She was awarded the 2022 Q21 artist residency in Vienna and has received numerous prizes, most notably the “Novica Tadić” Award (2020), the “Aladin Lukač” Award (2021), the “Anka Topić” Award (2021), the “Ratković Poetry Evenings” Award (2020), “Ulaznica 2021”, the “Mak Dizdar” Award (2018 and 2020), the “Milo Bošković” Award (2020 and 2023), and the Solin City Library Poetry Competition (2025).

Her work appears in Sun in the Water (Ukrainian: «Сонце у воді»), an anthology of contemporary Croatian and Bosnian-Herzegovinian poetry (2026).

Her poems have been translated into English, French, German, Italian, Polish, Hungarian, Ukrainian, and Hindi.

She lives and works in France.
